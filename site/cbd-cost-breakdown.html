Three Refinements Most Price Guides Skip

  1. Divide by mg, never ml. Bottle volume says nothing about CBD content; concentration varies up to 50x between bottles of identical size.
  2. Use the CoA, not just the label. Batch certificates of analysis report measured Total CBD. Dividing price by the verified number is the only lab-checked version of the math.
  3. Count servings you'll actually use. A bottle is only cheaper per used milligram if you finish it before it ages out.
