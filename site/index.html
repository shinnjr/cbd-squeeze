Is this really that simple?
Yes. The isolate is already dissolved in MCT oil at a known strength. You pour a measured amount, add your own MCT oil, and shake. No scale, no powder, no heating.
How much do I actually save?
A 1,000 mg tincture runs $40–$90 at retail. The same CBD from Simple Tinctures Concentrate costs about $6 in material. Most people save 80%+ over the year.
Does it contain THC?
No. It's pure CBD isolate — CBD with the other hemp compounds removed. Third-party lab tested at 0% THC (undetectable, well under 0.3%).

MCT oil (about $10 a liter on Amazon) and empty dropper bottles if you don't already have them. That's it.

People do, but CBD is not approved for animals, so we can't recommend or dose for them. Always talk to your veterinarian first.

10,000 mg makes about ten 1,000 mg tinctures. At one tincture a month, that's most of a year.
