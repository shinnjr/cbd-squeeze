What we test

Before any concentrate leaves our hands, every batch is sent to a third-party, ISO-accredited lab for a full panel:

  • Potency — confirms the exact mg of CBD per ml, so the measure-and-pour bottle is accurate.
  • THC — verifies the product is below 0.3% THC (CBD isolate is THC-free/undetectable).
  • Pesticides — screens for a broad panel of agricultural residues.
  • Heavy metals — lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury.
  • Residual solvents — ensures no processing solvents remain.
  • Microbials — bacteria, yeast, and mold.

Status: the first production batch certificate of analysis (COA) will be published on this page the moment it returns from the lab. Every bottle ships with a QR code that links to its COA.
