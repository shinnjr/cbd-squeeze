The polished brands with the sleek bottles and the influencer ads. You're paying mostly for marketing. A 1,000 mg bottle runs $150–200. Fine as an occasional splurge, terrible as a daily habit.

The cheapest finished bottles, often from bulk or white-label brands. Better, but you're still paying a 10x+ markup over the raw material for the convenience of a finished bottle.

2. Raw CBD isolate — $0.005–0.02/mg

The cheapest way, period. But it means weighing powder, dissolving into oil, and trusting your own measurements. Great if you enjoy the process; a hurdle if you just want your daily dose.
