Why store tinctures cost so much

Retail CBD oil sells for $0.067–0.20/mg because the price includes the label, the bottle, the marketing, the retailer's cut, and the brand's margin. The active ingredient — CBD isolate — is a commodity trading at $0.15–0.50 per gram wholesale. In a $100 bottle of 1,000 mg, the CBD itself is worth about $0.50.

Why raw isolate isn't the easy answer

Raw CBD isolate powder is the cheapest route — around $0.005–0.02/mg — but it means weighing powder, dissolving it into oil, and getting your measurements exactly right. Many people start there and give up. Concentrate removes that step: the isolate is already dissolved, and a measure-and-pour bottle handles the measuring.

The one-sentence takeaway

Buy finished tinctures and you pay $0.10–0.20/mg. Make your own with concentrate and you pay about $0.006/mg — an 80–90% savings for two minutes of work.
