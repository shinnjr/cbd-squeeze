Four Rules That Make Any CBD Purchase Cheaper

  1. Always compute price ÷ total mg. Sticker price is marketing; per-mg is truth.
  2. Buy concentration, not volume. Two same-sized bottles can differ 12x in CBD content.
  3. Demand the batch CoA. Untested cheap CBD is the most expensive kind when it's mislabeled.
  4. Stop paying for carrier oil twice. MCT oil is ~$8 at any grocery; premixed bottles charge extract prices for it.
